Which Dentrix reports

Mostly one report, plus a couple of easy lookups. Enter the totals-row numbers here.

Production, Collections & Doctor Collections

Provider A/R Totals report. Use the TOTAL row for practice-wide Production and Collections; use each doctor's own row, Collections column, for the by-doctor numbers.

Hygiene Collections

Same report: add up the hygienists' rows, Collections column (or use Dentrix's own hygiene total if it has one).

Treatment Plan Acceptance

Dentrix's Treatment Plan or Case Acceptance report, as a percent.

Patients & Add-Ons

New-patient count (Dentrix's New Patient report or dashboard) and production per add-on category (Ortho, Whitening, Implants, etc.), if you track those separately. Skip add-ons if you don't.

Attendance/tardiness comes separately from the doctor, and detailed aging/insurance reports still come to us as attachments. Neither is entered here.
